This policy explains what information Blok collects, why, and what you can do about it. Blok is run by [Your name or company name] ("we", "us"). If you have any questions, email [contact email].

The short version

  • If you use Blok without an account, everything stays on your device. Nothing is sent to us.
  • If you create an account, we store your email address and your tasks so they can sync between your devices. Nobody else can see them.
  • We don't show ads, sell your data, or use analytics or tracking.
  • You can export your tasks or delete your account and everything with it at any time, from the app.

Without an account

Your tasks, recurring tasks and settings are saved only on the device you're using. We never receive them.

With an account

When you sign in, we store:

  • Your email address, to identify your account and to send you account emails (confirming your email, resetting your password).
  • Your password, only in scrambled (hashed) form. We can't see or recover it; if you forget it, you set a new one.
  • Your tasks: their names, colours, times and lengths, which columns they're in, links between them, and your recurring tasks and their rules.
  • Two settings: the number of columns and how edits to recurring tasks apply by default.
  • Technical records: when each change was made (so the latest edit wins when two devices change the same task), and, as with any online service, short-lived server logs such as the IP address and device type used to sign in.

We use this information only to provide Blok: keeping your tasks the same on all your devices and running your account. The legal basis is that it's needed to provide the service you asked for.

Where it's kept and who can see it

Account data is stored by our hosting provider, Supabase, on servers in London, UK. Supabase processes it only on our instructions, under a data processing agreement. Where Supabase or its subprocessors handle any data outside the UK, they do so under the legal safeguards in that agreement.

Only you can see your tasks: each account can read only its own data. We don't share your information with anyone else, except where the law requires it.

These web pages are hosted by GitHub Pages, which may log the IP address of visitors for security purposes.

How long we keep it

  • Your account and tasks are kept until you delete them.
  • When you delete a task, a note that it was deleted is kept for up to 90 days so your other devices learn about it, then removed.
  • When you delete your account (Account › Delete account in the app), your account and all its tasks are deleted from our servers straight away. Copies may remain in our provider's backups for up to 7 days before they're overwritten.
  • Signing out doesn't delete anything from your account; the app asks whether to keep or remove the tasks on that device.

Your rights

You can, at any time:

  • See and take your data: Export in the app's menu saves all your tasks as a file.
  • Correct it: edit your tasks in the app.
  • Delete it: delete tasks, or your whole account, in the app.

You also have the right to ask us for a copy of your information, to restrict or object to how we use it, and to complain to the UK's Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk). Email [contact email] for anything the app doesn't let you do yourself.

Children

Blok isn't intended for children under 13, and we don't knowingly create accounts for them.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If a change affects how we use information you've already given us, we'll tell you in the app first.
